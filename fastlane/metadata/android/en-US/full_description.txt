Ratamatap helps beginners through advanced drummers practice snare-drum
rudiments on a snare or practice pad.

Follow falling notes in two hand lanes: blue for the left hand and red for the
right. Switch to sheet music to practice reading notation. Both views stay in
time with real snare samples and a metronome.

Practice features:
• All 40 standard rudiments, plus Timing basics lessons.
• Adjustable tempo, a count-in and looping.
• A speed trainer that raises the tempo after a set number of loops.
• Separate snare and metronome volume controls.
• Lead with the left hand by swapping the sticking.
• Favorites, remembered lesson settings and a practice history with the best
  tempo reached for each lesson.

Ratamatap is a play-along practice tool. It does not listen to or score your
playing. It works offline, with no ads, accounts or tracking.
