Ratamatap is an offline Android app for practicing snare rudiments.

This early development build shows a placeholder screen only. Lessons,
animated notation, synchronized drum playback, tempo controls and a practice
log are planned for v0.1. Microphone hit detection comes in a later release.

The app is free software under GPL-3.0-only, with no ads, analytics or
proprietary SDKs. The skeleton requests no runtime permissions. The app name and
application ID are provisional until the first public release.
